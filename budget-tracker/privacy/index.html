Budget Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 (ads and Premium added)

This policy explains what information the Budget Tracker app ("Budget Tracker", "the app") uses, and why. Budget Tracker is published on Google Play by WavesElevate ("we", "us"). In short: your money data stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to advertisers. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play, and we do not run any server that receives your data.

1. Information stored on your phone only

Everything you enter is saved on your phone and never sent to us. This includes your name (if you type one), your transactions, accounts and balances, categories, budgets, savings goals, bills and subscriptions, and your settings such as language and currency. Deleting the app, or tapping "Delete all data" in Settings, erases all of it.

2. PIN and biometric lock

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value in your phone's secure storage. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by your phone's operating system; the app only receives a yes or no answer and never sees your fingerprint or face data.

3. Notifications

If you turn on bill reminders, budget alerts or a daily reminder, the app schedules them on your phone. No push service or server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

4. Backups and exports

You can export a backup file or a CSV spreadsheet of your transactions. The file is created on your phone and goes only where you choose to save or share it (for example your files, email or a cloud drive you pick). Importing a backup reads a file you select. We never receive these files.

5. Smart assistant

The assistant answers questions about your money by reading your data on your phone. Your questions and data are not sent to any server or AI service.

6. Ads (free version)

The free version shows small banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this to show personalised ads, where you have allowed it. Your budget data, transactions and balances are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change it later in Settings > Ads > Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Google settings.
  • Buying Premium removes all ads; the ads SDK is then not started.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Premium purchase

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Premium, and stores that yes or no answer on your phone. Google's own Payments privacy notice applies to the payment.

8. What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in in this version.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • No connection to your bank. The app only knows the amounts you type in.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. Your money data never leaves your phone unless you export it.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, location or other files.

If we later add optional online accounts or anything else that changes this, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play listing before that version is released.

9. Children

Budget Tracker is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Your choices

You can turn off notifications, change your ad consent, remove the app lock, export or reset your data, or delete the app at any time. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete; if you have a question, contact us below.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
