Fun Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Fun Games app ("Fun Games", "the app") uses. Fun Games is published on Google Play by WavesElevate ("we", "us"). In short: Fun Games works completely offline, has no accounts, shows no ads, and does not collect or share any personal data.

1. Information stored on your phone only

To run the games, the app saves a few things on your phone:

  • the player names you type in and their scores,
  • your chosen language and content types (family-friendly, funny, brain teasers),
  • the timer length you set for each game.

This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. You can remove players and reset scores in the app, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything.

2. Information we collect

None. Fun Games does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: the phone vibrates briefly when a timer ends.
  • Keep screen on: the screen stays on while a game is running, so it does not lock in the middle of a round.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

4. Children

Fun Games is a family-friendly party game for groups. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not collect your data, so we do not share or sell it.

6. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that use data, such as ads, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
