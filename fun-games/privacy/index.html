Fun Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 (ads added)

This policy explains what information the Fun Games app ("Fun Games", "the app") uses. Fun Games is published on Google Play by WavesElevate ("we", "us"). In short: the games work offline and have no accounts. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device data to show and measure those ads. We do not collect any personal data ourselves.

1. Information stored on your phone only

To run the games, the app saves a few things on your phone:

  • the player names you type in and their scores,
  • your chosen language and content types (family-friendly, funny, brain teasers),
  • the timer length you set for each game.

This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. You can remove players and reset scores in the app, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything.

2. Information we collect

We (WavesElevate) do not collect any personal information. We have no server, no analytics and no crash reporting of our own.

3. Ads from Google AdMob

Fun Games is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob. A banner appears on the menu screens and, from time to time, a full-screen ad appears after a game. Ads are never shown in the middle of a turn. When your phone is online, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your phone's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address,
  • information about ad views and taps, and app performance and crash data related to ads.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time with "Ad privacy choices" at the bottom of the home screen. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Ads need the internet; with the internet off, the games still work and no ads load.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads, as described above.
  • Vibration: the phone vibrates briefly when a timer ends.
  • Keep screen on: the screen stays on while a game is running.

The app does not use your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

5. Children

Fun Games is a party game for groups of teens and adults, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. The only data sharing is the ad data collected by Google AdMob described in section 3.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version uses data in a new way, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
