Sajda Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Sajda app ("Sajda", "the app") uses, and why. Sajda is published on Google Play by WavesElevate ("we", "us"). In short: Sajda has no accounts, shows no ads, and we do not run any server that receives your data.

1. Information stored on your phone only

Your settings are saved on your phone and never sent to us. This includes your chosen city or saved location, prayer calculation method and time adjustments, alert settings, language and theme, your tasbih counts, custom dhikr and your Quran reading position. Deleting the app, or tapping "Reset the app" in Settings, erases all of it.

2. Location

Sajda asks for your location (only while you use the app) so it can calculate prayer times, show the Qibla direction and find nearby mosques. You can refuse and pick your city by hand instead; the app works either way. Sajda never uses your location in the background.

  • Prayer times and Qibla are calculated on your phone. Your location is not sent anywhere for this.
  • City name: to show the name of the place you are in, the app uses your phone's built-in geocoding service (on Android this is provided by Google), which receives your coordinates.
  • Nearby mosques: when you open the Mosques tab, the app sends your saved location to public OpenStreetMap servers (Overpass API) to look up mosques around you, and loads map images from OpenStreetMap. These requests are made only to show you the results and are not stored by us.

3. Notifications

If you turn on prayer alerts, Sajda schedules them on your phone. No push service or server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

4. Content downloads

The Arabic Quran, the English translation and the hadith collections are built into the app. If you choose another translation language, the app downloads that translation from the jsDelivr content network. Map files come from OpenStreetMap and unpkg. Like any website, these services see your IP address when your phone connects to them. Their own privacy policies apply: OpenStreetMap Foundation, jsDelivr.

5. What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads, no advertising ID and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or files.

If we ever add ads or anything else that changes this, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play listing before that version is released.

6. Children

Sajda is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your choices

You can deny or remove location permission, turn off notifications, or reset or delete the app at any time. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete; if you have a question, contact us below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
